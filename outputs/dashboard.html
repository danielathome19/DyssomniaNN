Clinical Telemetry Reference & Polysomnography Parameter Guide
AASM Guidelines • Dual-Process Biomarkers
Total Sleep Time (TST) & Sleep %

Cumulative duration of objective non-wake epochs (N1, N2, N3, REM). Sleep Fraction (Env) calculates the percentage of total environmental observation time spent asleep.

Well-Rested Index (0-100%)

Composite clinical restoration score evaluating homeostatic clearance (Process S), sleep quota attainment, N3 slow-wave / REM architecture, and WASO continuity.

WASO & Ultradian Cycles

Wake After Sleep Onset measures nocturnal awakenings during the sleep period (excluding daytime wakefulness). Cycles count completed ~90-minute NREM-REM cycles.

Circadian Offset (CRSWD)

Horizontal phase shift (+4.0h in Delayed Phase). The biological sleep gate does not open until ~03:00, where morning sunlight (06:30) forces early awakening and chronic sleep debt.

Cortical Hyperarousal (Paradoxical)

Attenuated cortical GABAergic inhibition (gain 0.25) maintains waking Beta/Gamma power (>15 Hz) during slow-wave sleep, causing subjective feeling of being awake despite objective sleep.

SOREMPs & Orexin Deficit (Narcolepsy)

Sleep-Onset REM Periods (REM within 15 min of onset) and daytime microsleep attacks caused by severe (>90%) loss of hypothalamic orexin/hypocretin wake-stabilizing tone.
